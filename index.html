Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.2

At Campfire AirDrop, we prioritize your privacy while providing location-aware social experiences. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and protect your information.

Key Takeaway: We do not store historic physical location logs. Your coordinates are used in volatile memory solely to calculate distance relative to nearby drops.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Username, password hash, and email hash (SHA-256 encrypted).
  • Precise Location Data: GPS coordinates collected while radar is active to calculate nearby drops within 100 meters.
  • Generated Content: Text status messages ("AirDrops") submitted while broadcasting.
  • Device Metadata: Basic device identifiers required for secure token validation.

2. How Information is Used

  • To render local AirDrop streams relative to your current location.
  • To enforce community safety guidelines using automated 3−strike moderation and AI filters.
  • To limit spam activities (e.g., daily 10−drop limit for free accounts).

3. Data Retention & Expiry

AirDrops automatically self-destruct from active memory after 10 minutes. Unused location pings are automatically purged after 30 minutes of inactivity.

4. Data Erasure & Account Deletion

You can request complete erasure of your profile and data at any time through the in-app settings or by contacting badger.developer.001@gmail.com.
